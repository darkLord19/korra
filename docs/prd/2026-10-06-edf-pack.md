## 1. Context

From **1 Oct 2026**, under RBI's Foreign Exchange Management (Export and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2026, every Indian service exporter must file a monthly **Export Declaration Form (EDF)** with their AD bank within 30 days of month-end. That includes freelancers and agencies, and it replaces SOFTEX for software exporters. Exports are then tracked in EDPMS until the money is realised: within 9 months, or 12 months for INR invoices.

- **First deadline:** 30 Nov 2026, for October invoices.
- **Submission:** through the AD bank's portal or RM. The method varies by bank, and there is no public API.
- **Competitors:** payment rails (Skydo, Razorpay MoneySaver, Infinity, Xflow) each cover only their own flows. Skydo's EDF tool is "being built". Razorpay offers guidance, and Infinity coordinates with bank RMs. Variabl is EDF-focused, with CAs doing the filing and no AI.

**Strategy (from the 7 Powers analysis):** be **rail-neutral**, which no rail can copy without hurting its own volume, and use that position to become the exporter's **system of record**, which creates switching costs. Freelancers are the acquisition wedge. CA firms and agencies are the paying market, since the ₹20 Cr ARR floor needs around ₹30k/yr per account.

## 2. Problem

A freelancer or small agency billing foreign clients:
- doesn't know the new EDF exists, what fields it needs, or how their bank wants it;
- gets paid through 1–3 rails and must match each invoice to a payment and its FIRA by hand;
- has no view of which invoices are still unrealised and approaching the 9-month limit;
- pays a CA ₹1–3k/month, or does it alone and risks FEMA non-compliance.

Their rail helps only with payments that went through that rail.

## 3. Target user (v1)

**Primary:** an Indian freelancer, or an agency of 1–10 people, with foreign clients, paid via one or more of Skydo, Payoneer, Wise, Infinity, Xflow, PayPal or a direct bank SWIFT transfer.

**Secondary (observed, not served in v1):** the CA who files on their behalf.

## 4. Goals and non-goals

**Goals**
1. A user produces a correct, bank-ready EDF pack for a month in **under 15 minutes**, whichever rails they use.
2. The user always knows which exports are unrealised and when each deadline falls.
3. Build a lead list of CAs who serve several exporters.

**Non-goals (v1)**
- Submitting the EDF on the user's behalf, or integrating with bank APIs
- GST (LUT, GSTR-1, refunds), income tax, TDS
- Rail-cost comparison (planned for phase 2)
- A CA-firm workspace or billing (phase 3)
- Goods exports

## 5. Feature 1: Monthly EDF Pack (wedge)

### User flow
1. **Onboard** (one-time): PAN, GSTIN, IEC (optional), legal name and address, AD bank(s), default SAC code(s).
2. **Upload** for the month:
   - invoices (PDF/image)
   - rail statements / FIRAs (PDF/CSV) from any rail
3. **Review:** AI extracts the fields and proposes invoice ↔ payment matches. The user confirms or fixes them. Low-confidence items are flagged and must be resolved before export.
4. **Generate** one EDF pack per AD bank for the month.
5. **Submit** (outside the product), following the in-app bank-specific guide. The user then marks the pack as submitted and can upload the bank acknowledgement.

### Fields extracted

| Level | Fields |
|---|---|
| Exporter profile | Name, address, PAN, GSTIN, IEC, AD bank and AD code, mode of receipt |
| Per invoice | Recipient name and address, country, invoice no. and date, currency, amount, net realisable value, contract reference (if any), service description, SAC code |
| Per payment | Rail, date, foreign currency amount, INR credited, FX rate, fees, FIRA/remittance reference, purpose code |

### Output (per bank, per month)
- Filled EDF form (PDF)
- Spreadsheet with the bank's column order (ICICI, HDFC and Axis in v1; generic layout for other banks)
- Supporting documents as a single zip (invoices and FIRAs)
- A "How to submit at {bank}" guide

### Matching rules
- Candidates are scored on amount (after rail fees and FX tolerance), currency, client name, and the date window.
- One invoice can match several partial payments, and one payment can cover several invoices.
- Unpaid invoices are still declared, because the EDF is filed by invoice month and not by payment. They go to the tracker as open.

### Acceptance criteria
- A user with 5 invoices across 2 rails produces a pack in under 15 minutes (median, measured in beta).
- Field extraction is at least 95% correct on the beta document set before public launch. Every field stays editable.
- No pack can be generated while a required field is empty or a flagged item is unresolved.
- The pack opens correctly in Excel and Google Sheets, and the PDF prints on A4.

## 6. Feature 2: Realisation Tracker (retention)

- Every declared invoice gets a realisation deadline: invoice date + 9 months, or + 12 months for INR-invoiced exports.
- Statuses: Open → Partially realised → Realised (matched payment / eBRC) → Overdue.
- Dashboard: total outstanding, due within 60 days, overdue.
- Notifications (email; WhatsApp in v1.1):
  - "Your {month} EDF is due on {date}", sent 10 days and 3 days before
  - "Invoice {no.} realisation deadline in 60 / 30 days"
- **Why it matters:** this ledger of open export bills is the start of the system of record and of switching costs.

## 7. Feature 3: Share with my CA (bridge)

- The user invites a CA by email. The CA gets **read and download** access to that client's packs and tracker.
- The CA sees every client who invited them in one simple list.
- Internally, track the number of CAs with 3 or more clients. These CAs are the lead list for the phase-3 paid product for practices.

## 8. Pricing (v1)

| Plan | Price | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Free | ₹0 | Up to 10 invoices/month, 1 AD bank, tracker, 1 CA share |
| Pro | ~₹299/month (₹2,999/yr) | Unlimited invoices, multiple banks, audit document bundle, priority support |

The CA-firm plan is out of scope for this PRD. The phase-3 target is around ₹30k+/yr per firm.

**Neutrality rule:** no referral or revenue-share deals with any payment rail in year one.

## 10. Metrics

**North star:** monthly active filers, meaning users who generated an EDF pack for a given month.

| Metric | Target by 2027-03-31 |
|---|---|
| Users filing two or more consecutive months | 2,000 |
| **Users with two or more rails connected (kill metric)** | **≥30% of paying users.** Below this by month 6 → pivot to the CA channel or partner with a rail. |
| Free → Pro conversion | ≥5% |
| CAs with three or more clients on the platform | 100 |
| Matches accepted without user edits | ≥85% |

## 11. Data, security and legal

- **Sensitive data:** PAN, GSTIN, client names, bank details and invoices.
- Data stored in India and encrypted at rest and in transit. Deleted within 30 days of a user request.
- User documents are not used for model training. LLM providers are used under zero-retention terms.
- Clear disclaimer: the product prepares documents and the user or their CA submits them. It is not legal or tax advice.
- Audit log of every edit to extracted fields.

## 12. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Bank formats are unknown or vary by branch | Week-1 format collection. Generic PDF fallback. Keep the guide per bank. |
| Skydo or Razorpay ship free EDF filing | Lean on multi-rail users. Ship the tracker and CA sharing fast so the record is already in our product. |
| Regulatory detail changes (RBI FAQs, amendments) | Keep form templates in versioned config. Watch RBI notifications weekly. |
| Extraction errors cause wrong filings | Mandatory review step. Confidence flags. Block generation on unresolved items. |
| Freelancers won't pay | Expected. Free tier exists to acquire users and data. Revenue comes from CA firms in phase 3. |

## 13. Roadmap after v1

- **Phase 2 (by 2027-03-31):** neutral rail-cost report; GST export module (LUT renewal before 31 Mar, zero-rated GSTR-1 data) via a GSP partner.
- **Phase 3 (by 2027-09-30):** paid CA-firm workspace with many clients, eBRC auto-matching, audit packs for FEMA, GST refund and Form 67 notices.
